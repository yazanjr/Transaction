About Transocean

Built on relationships. Sustained by reliability.

Transocean Trading and Oilfield Supply is a Jordanian company specialized in supplying materials to the drilling and oilfield industry.

For approximately twenty years, Potassium Chloride of Jordanian origin has been a central part of the company's business. Over that period, Transocean has developed commercial relationships across the drilling and drilling-fluids supply chain and served customers across regional and international markets.

The company continues to expand its material portfolio while remaining focused on the principles that built those relationships: responsiveness, dependable supply and straightforward commercial cooperation.
